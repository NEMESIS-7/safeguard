## Lead to examine next

**Merchant funds safeguarding.** Dated, specific, mandatory, with identifiable buyers (64 licensed payment service providers at end 2025, per the [Bank of Ghana payments report](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Payment-Systems-Annual-Report-2025-1.pdf)), plus settlement banks.

Check before believing it:

1. Read the notice itself on bog.gov.gh.
2. Search for vendors selling safeguarding or float reconciliation in Ghana and elsewhere.
3. Find out how providers prove compliance today.
4. Assess whether a new builder can sell to regulated firms. A realistic first route may be a portfolio-grade system that wins a job or a partnership.
